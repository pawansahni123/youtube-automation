Privacy Policy

App name: Wild Facts Daily Automation
Last updated: 2026

Wild Facts Daily Automation ("the App") is a personal-use automation tool that generates and uploads video content to a single YouTube channel that the developer owns and operates. This policy explains how the App uses Google account data.

What data we access

The App requests permission to upload videos to, and read basic performance analytics for, the YouTube channel(s) explicitly authorized by the account owner. This includes:

  • Uploading video files, titles, descriptions, tags, and thumbnails to the authorized channel.
  • Reading aggregate view/watch-time/engagement statistics (YouTube Analytics) for videos uploaded by the App.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not access, read, or store any personal data belonging to other Google or YouTube users.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any data obtained through this authorization to third parties.
  • We do not use the data for advertising or any purpose other than operating the authorized channel.

Data storage

OAuth credentials are stored securely as encrypted secrets in the developer's private automation environment and are never exposed publicly. Video metadata and analytics summaries used to inform future content are stored privately for the developer's own reference.

Data retention and deletion

Authorization can be revoked at any time by the account owner via Google Account permissions. Revoking access immediately stops the App's ability to act on the account.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer at the email address associated with this project's Google Cloud account.

This is a personal, single-user automation project, not a commercial product offered to the public.
